Winner's curse in the held-out frontier (160 replicates, half size 310)

  subsets dominating the rule, judged on a held-out half : median 72
  subsets dominating the rule, judged at the population  : median 31
  rule on the EMPIRICAL held-out frontier                : 0.056 [0.025, 0.094]
  rule on the POPULATION frontier                        : 0.106 [0.062, 0.156]

  of the subsets that dominate on half A:
    still dominate on an independent half B              : 0.376 [0.324, 0.431]
    genuinely dominate at the population                 : 0.195 [0.162, 0.230]

  The empirical frontier is an argmax over 512 noisy estimates, so it sits
  below the population frontier and the count of dominating subsets is
  inflated. The ratio of the two medians is the optimism factor a reader
  should apply when reading the corresponding numbers in the two cohorts.
