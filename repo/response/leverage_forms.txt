So the attenuation factor is form-independent up to sign and a constant.
It is attenuation by a known factor, NOT disappearance: the contrast is
shrunk, and whether the shrunken contrast is detectable remains a question
of effect size and sample size. Only an exactly zero factor -- the logical
implication case -- makes a cell structurally unidentifiable.

Prediction accuracy: the AND coefficient is accurate to a few per cent;
the OR and XOR coefficients are over-predicted by 10-30%% because the
derivation ignores the reweighting and non-collapsibility that operate when
the bonus reaches a large share of the cohort. They are order-of-magnitude
guides, not exact.
